How to use this app
  • Choose test: Use the Select test dropdown in the top right.
  • Switch sections: Click tabs Part 1 through Part 8.
  • Answer: Click gaps for multiple choice (Part 1), type into boxes (Parts 2–4), or choose options on the right (Parts 5–8).
  • Evaluate: Click Check to color-code answers, Calculate Percentage for diagnostic accuracy, or Clue for hints.
How scoring works in C1 Advanced

• Compensatory scoring: You do not need to pass every individual part. Cambridge calculates your final grade by averaging five equal areas (Reading, Use of English, Writing, Listening, Speaking).

• Overall pass target: A total average score of ~60% (180+ on the Cambridge English Scale) awards a C1 Pass (Grade C). Higher sections balance out lower sections!
